Questions & Answers
Frequently Asked Questions
How do I register for an academy batch? +
Submit an inquiry using the contact form below or visit one of our campus offices. Once fee installment is confirmed, your student portal account credentials will be issued.
Can I access class recordings if I miss a session? +
Yes. Recorded lectures and lecture notes are uploaded to the corresponding topic in your student portal within hours of the class finishing.
How do students submit homework assignments? +
Students upload digital files (PDF, image, Word) inside the relevant course topic. Once graded by the instructor, marks and comments appear directly in your portal.
How do online classes work? +
Scheduled live classes include a secure meeting link accessible through your Schedule and Course pages. Live join links become active around class start times.
Are fee installment plans available? +
Yes. Batch tuition fees can be structured in installments. Installment due dates and payment receipts are tracked in the student portal under Fee Details.
