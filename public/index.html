FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
How do I enroll in a batch? +
Visit our office or contact us below. You'll be placed in a batch based on your target test and start date, then receive login credentials once your fee is confirmed.
Can I watch lectures if I miss a class? +
Yes - all live classes are recorded and uploaded to your student portal within 24 hours. You'll receive a notification when the recording is available.
How are assignments submitted? +
Directly through the student portal by uploading your file (PDF, image, or Word) inside the relevant topic's Assignment tab. You'll see your submission status and grade once marked.
Will I be notified about deadlines? +
Yes. The portal sends notifications when material is uploaded, when an assignment is due, and escalating warnings if the deadline is approaching and you haven't submitted.
Can I contact my teacher directly? +
Yes - every course page has a "Message Teacher" button that lets you send a message directly to your subject teacher for any academic questions.
Is online study available? +
We offer both physical and online classes. Online students access the same recorded lectures, notes, quizzes, and assignments through the portal.
